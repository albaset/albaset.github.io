About

Who I Am

I'm a final-year Mechanical Engineering student at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), graduating in July 2026 with a CGPA of 3.77 out of 4.00. I have a strong interest in materials engineering, design, manufacturing, and computational tools.

My final-year thesis investigates SiC and fly ash reinforcement in hybrid Al-Cu metal matrix composites, combining hands-on fabrication (stir casting) with mechanical and corrosion testing. I've also spent time on the ground at one of Bangladesh's largest power plants and at manufacturing facilities, which shapes how I think about design for real processes and real operating conditions.

I aspire to build a career in materials and mechanical engineering research, and I am currently exploring graduate study opportunities where I can apply engineering principles to solve practical problems.

To apply engineering knowledge, hands-on fabrication skill, and analytical problem-solving toward developing practical, sustainable materials solutions — while continuing to grow as a researcher.
