Summary

§ 01

I'm a final-year Mechanical Engineering student at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), graduating in July 2026 with a CGPA of 3.77 out of 4.

My final-year thesis investigates SiC and fly ash reinforcement in hybrid Al-Cu metal matrix composites, combining hands-on fabrication (stir casting) with mechanical and corrosion testing. Alongside research, I've spent time on the ground at one of Bangladesh's largest power plants and at manufacturing facilities, which shapes how I think about design: for real processes, real constraints, and real operating conditions.

I design and model in SolidWorks, compute in MATLAB, FORTRAN and C, and I'm currently active in student leadership through the CUET Career Club and the CUET Automotive & Mobility Society.
